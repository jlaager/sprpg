Anchor Drop Falls
System: Pirate Borg (Dark Fantasy Pirates) | Seats: 5 | GM: Dillon
Experience Level: Some Experience Helpful
Character Creation: Character creation will be done at the table at the event.
Content Notes: Colonialism, Death, Diseases, Dismemberment, Sea monsters, Drug Use/Addiction, Skeletons/zombies/eldritch horrors, high chance of character death, Piracy
The year is 1666, it is an age of war, an age of death, an age of PIRACY. A great rift known as The Abyss has torn open in the middle of the Caribbean, monsters and eldritch horrors spill from its maw. Ships sent to investigate are never seen again, and what few survivors are found, are driven mad babbling about The Great Old Ones, and the horrific terrors they control. Towns and settlements are laid to waste as the dead rise from their graves, feasting on the flesh of their old brethren. The destroyed undead produce a highly addictive substance known as Ash, giving great strength to those who use it, but at a sometimes devastating cost. The nations of England, France, and Spain vie for control over the ravaged dark Caribbean. Their forces spread thin, they employ anyone they can, mercenaries and pirates alike. The world is teetering on a knifes edge, and a simple nudge could send the entire planet into oblivion.

You are a pirate, a low life, a scoundrel. Through either fate, bad luck, or a combination of both, you find yourself in the Town of Salt Port. A fledgling portside city on the outskirts of Tortuga. Once an honest fishing village, it is now run by Fugitives and Deserters of Nations from around the world. Word has traveled around town of a Pirate Captain seeking to put together a crew. He goes by the name of Captain Mathias Skand, but around pirates, he is known as The Abyss Touched. Not much is known of him, only that he has journeyed to the rift, and has lived to tell the tale. He speaks of ancient, and powerful relics that reside within, and seeks to find them for himself. He promises power and immense riches to any brave, or foolhardy enough to join him on his journey.

Word has spread of a centuries old pirate treasure that lies abandoned in a hidden cave behind a waterfall. It is said that a village of Deep One have taken over the cave and made it their home. If they can be cleared out, or run off, the treasures are yours for the taking.

Do you have what it takes to prove yourself a pirate??

Pirate Borg is a violent, and grim dark game and setting. story lines, and character creation can have aspects of body horror that could make some players uncomfortable. Aspects and character traits developed during character creation can be rerolled or changed if players do not wish to keep them.

Experience Level: No Experience Required
Character Creation: Pregenerated characters provided. Outside characters are not used for this adventure.
Content Notes: Body horror, insect infestation
The temple-school of Het Thamsya is in mortal peril. Its founder, Satur, is nearing the end of a decade-long deep meditation to unlock the secrets of the Automata Path. However, a colossal, monstrously expanding giant wasp nest is devouring the back of the complex, and Satur's literal-minded mechanical guardians have locked down the temple to keep all intruders out.
