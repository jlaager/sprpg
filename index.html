Sun Prairie RPG

Sun Prairie RPG is a free, community-focused tabletop RPG group for adults in Sun Prairie, Wisconsin and the greater Madison area.

Whether you're a longtime player or completely new to tabletop roleplaying games, you're welcome at our table. We're here to help people learn new games, meet fellow gamers, and find groups to play with.

What to Expect

  • Free tabletop RPG events
  • Beginner-friendly games and opportunities to learn new systems
  • Pregenerated characters are provided for most games
  • A welcoming, inclusive tabletop community for adults

  • OSRIC (AD&D 1e)
  • Mutant Crawl Classics (MCC)

And we're always interested in trying new systems if there's a GM willing to teach them! Check out our About Us page for more information about running a game at Sun Prairie RPG.
