Offworld Mining
System: D&D 5e | Seats: 6 | GM: Sam
Experience Level: No Experience Required
Character Creation: Pregenerated characters provided. Outside characters are not used for this adventure.
Six months ago, an ancient gate was discovered outside the city of Jemorra, opening once a month to a mysterious new world. Explorers returned with strange treasures, glittering minerals, and tales of breathtaking lands untouched by civilization.

The king quickly sent settlers through the gate to establish a mining town. But when the portal opened again, no one came back.

Now, the king needs adventurers willing to step through the gate, find the missing settlers, and uncover what happened to them. There may even be treasure waiting for you...

Will you answer the call and venture into the unknown?
